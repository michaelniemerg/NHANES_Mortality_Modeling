

================================================================================
  BACKLOG - THEORY 3: ADJUSTED CDC TABLE AS EXPECTED BASIS
================================================================================

  Added: April 2026
  Status: Pending
  Prerequisite: Script 13 (cdc_vs_actual_comparison.py) - COMPLETE

  Concept:
    Replace the GAM baselines with an adjusted CDC mortality table
    throughout the pipeline. The adjusted table = CDC qx * A/E factor
    by age band (and optionally by gender and duration).

  Findings from Script 13:
    - Overall NHANES A/E vs CDC = 0.988 (very close to 1.0)
    - Duration 1 A/E = 0.890 (healthy participant effect)
    - Ages 30-39: A/E around 0.55 (dramatic healthy volunteer bias)
    - Ages 50-65: A/E around 0.95 (close to population)
    - Ages 80-84: A/E = 1.18 (above CDC, possibly top-coding effect)

  Implementation options:
    A) Single overall factor (0.988) applied to all CDC qx
       - Simplest; barely changes anything
    B) Age-band factors applied to CDC qx
       - Captures the age-dependent selection bias
       - Most impact on younger ages
    C) Age-band x gender x duration factors
       - Most granular; captures duration-1 selection effect
       - Risk of overfitting with small cells

  Impact on pipeline:
    - Scripts 04 (GAM baselines) could be eliminated
    - Script 06 would use adjusted CDC table for baseline lookups
    - Script 09 relativities would be vs adjusted CDC, not raw CDC
    - All relative mortality charts would update
    - Mortality table builder (script 11) would use adjusted CDC
      as the population table for grading

  Decision needed:
    Which level of granularity (A, B, or C) to use?
    Recommendation: Option B (age-band factors) is the best balance
    of accuracy and stability. It captures the main pattern without
    the noise of small cells in option C.
