================================================================================
  NHANES 1999-2018 Combined Dataset with Linked Mortality
  Data Dictionary
================================================================================

  Source:    CDC National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES)
  Cycles:   1999-2000 through 2017-2018 (10 two-year cycles)
  Rows:     101,316 participants
  Columns:  33
  File:     nhanes_all_cycles_with_mortality.csv

  Mortality linkage from the National Death Index (NDI), with
  follow-up through December 31, 2019.


================================================================================
  IDENTIFIERS
================================================================================

  SEQN            Unique participant ID within each NHANES cycle.
                  Use SEQN + CYCLE together to uniquely identify a person
                  across the full combined dataset.

  CYCLE           Survey cycle label (e.g. "1999-2000", "2017-2018").
                  Added during data combination; not an original NHANES variable.


================================================================================
  DEMOGRAPHICS
================================================================================

  RIDAGEYR        Age in years at screening.
                  Range: 0-85. Ages 85+ are top-coded to 85.

  RIAGENDR        Gender.
                  1 = Male
                  2 = Female

  RIDRETH1        Race/ethnicity (5 categories).
                  1 = Mexican American
                  2 = Other Hispanic
                  3 = Non-Hispanic White
                  4 = Non-Hispanic Black
                  5 = Other Race / Multi-Racial

  DMDEDUC2        Education level (adults 20+).
                  1 = Less than 9th grade
                  2 = 9-11th grade (no diploma)
                  3 = High school graduate / GED
                  4 = Some college or AA degree
                  5 = College graduate or above
                  7 = Refused
                  9 = Don't know

  INDFMPIR        Ratio of family income to poverty threshold.
                  Range: 0-5. Values above 5 are top-coded to 5.
                  Higher values indicate higher income relative to poverty.


================================================================================
  BODY MEASUREMENTS
================================================================================

  BMXBMI          Body Mass Index (kg/m^2).
                  Calculated from measured weight and height.

  BMXWT           Weight in kilograms.

  BMXHT           Standing height in centimeters.

  BMXWAIST        Waist circumference in centimeters.
                  Measured at the iliac crest.


================================================================================
  BLOOD PRESSURE
================================================================================

  BPXSY1          Systolic blood pressure, 1st reading (mmHg).
  BPXDI1          Diastolic blood pressure, 1st reading (mmHg).
  BPXSY2          Systolic blood pressure, 2nd reading (mmHg).
  BPXDI2          Diastolic blood pressure, 2nd reading (mmHg).

                  Up to 4 readings were taken; only the first 2 are
                  included here. Common practice is to average available
                  readings for analysis.


================================================================================
  LABORATORY VALUES
================================================================================

  LBXTC           Total cholesterol (mg/dL).
                  Measured from blood specimen at the MEC exam.

  LBXGH           Glycohemoglobin / HbA1c (%).
                  Reflects average blood glucose over prior 2-3 months.
                  Values >= 6.5% are a diagnostic criterion for diabetes.


================================================================================
  SMOKING
================================================================================

  SMQ020          Smoked at least 100 cigarettes in life.
                  1 = Yes
                  2 = No
                  7 = Refused
                  9 = Don't know

  SMQ040          Current smoking status (asked only if SMQ020 = 1).
                  1 = Every day
                  2 = Some days
                  3 = Not at all (former smoker)


================================================================================
  ALCOHOL USE
================================================================================

  ALQ101          Had at least 12 alcoholic drinks in past year.
                  1 = Yes
                  2 = No

  ALQ120Q         How often drank alcohol in past 12 months.
                  Numeric count; units vary by cycle (days per week,
                  month, or year depending on follow-up question).


================================================================================
  DIABETES
================================================================================

  DIQ010          Doctor told you that you have diabetes.
                  1 = Yes
                  2 = No
                  3 = Borderline
                  7 = Refused
                  9 = Don't know


================================================================================
  MEDICAL CONDITIONS (self-reported, ever diagnosed)
================================================================================

  MCQ160B         Ever told you had congestive heart failure (CHF).
                  1 = Yes
                  2 = No

  MCQ160C         Ever told you had coronary heart disease (CHD).
                  1 = Yes
                  2 = No

  MCQ160F         Ever told you had a stroke.
                  1 = Yes
                  2 = No

  MCQ220          Ever told you had cancer or a malignancy.
                  1 = Yes
                  2 = No


================================================================================
  MORTALITY OUTCOME (from CDC Linked Mortality File)
================================================================================

  ELIGSTAT        Mortality linkage eligibility.
                  1 = Eligible (adult 18+ with sufficient ID data)
                  2 = Under age 18, ineligible for public-use file
                  3 = Ineligible, insufficient identifying data

                  NOTE: Only participants with ELIGSTAT = 1 should be
                  used for survival analysis. Filter on this first.

  MORTSTAT        Vital status (for ELIGSTAT = 1 only).
                  0 = Presumed alive (did not match NDI through 2019)
                  1 = Presumed deceased (matched to NDI death record)

  PERMTH_INT      Person-months of follow-up from NHANES interview date.
                  For deceased (MORTSTAT=1): months from interview to death.
                  For alive (MORTSTAT=0): months from interview to Dec 31, 2019.
                  This is the TIME variable for survival models.

  PERMTH_EXM      Person-months of follow-up from MEC examination date.
                  Same concept as PERMTH_INT but clock starts at the
                  physical exam rather than the household interview.

  UCOD_LEADING    Underlying cause of death (leading cause categories).
                  Only populated for deceased participants (MORTSTAT = 1).
                   1 = Diseases of the heart
                   2 = Malignant neoplasms (cancer)
                   3 = Chronic lower respiratory diseases
                   4 = Accidents (unintentional injuries)
                   5 = Cerebrovascular diseases (stroke)
                   6 = Alzheimer's disease
                   7 = Diabetes mellitus
                   8 = Influenza and pneumonia
                   9 = Nephritis / nephrotic syndrome / nephrosis
                  10 = All other causes

  DIABETES_MORT   Diabetes listed as a cause of death.
                  0 = No
                  1 = Yes

  HYPERTEN_MORT   Hypertension listed as a cause of death.
                  0 = No
                  1 = Yes


================================================================================
  NOTES FOR ANALYSIS
================================================================================

  Missing values     Blank/NaN values occur when a participant was not
                     eligible for a question (e.g. age-restricted items),
                     declined to answer, or did not complete the exam.

  Survival analysis  Filter to ELIGSTAT == 1 first. Use MORTSTAT as the
                     event indicator and PERMTH_INT as the time variable.

  Combining cycles   Earlier cycles (e.g. 1999-2000) have longer follow-up
                     but the same censoring date. The CYCLE column lets
                     you filter or stratify by survey wave.

  Survey weights     This file does not include NHANES sample weights.
                     Unweighted analyses are valid for prediction modeling.
                     For nationally representative prevalence estimates,
                     download the appropriate weight variables from the
                     DEMO files on the CDC website.

  Perturbation       The public-use mortality file has had some follow-up
                     times and cause-of-death codes replaced with synthetic
                     values to protect participant privacy. Vital status
                     (MORTSTAT) was NOT perturbed.

  Citation           National Center for Health Statistics. Continuous
                     NHANES Public-use Linked Mortality Files, 2019.
                     Hyattsville, Maryland. Available from:
                     https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data-linkage/mortality-public.htm
